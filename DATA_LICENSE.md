# OpenStreetMap data and licences

The road and pedestrian geometry/attributes in `vehicles.js` and `walking.js` originate from **OpenStreetMap contributors**. Data was queried through QuickOSM/QGIS and first exported through qgis2web; unused attributes were removed to reduce download size. Feature geometries and available `name` and `highway` values were preserved.
